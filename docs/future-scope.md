
# RoadGuard - Future Scope

RoadGuard has several opportunities for future development.

## Future Enhancements

1. **Real-Time Monitoring:** Improve live road hazard detection.
2. **AI Accuracy:** Improve pothole detection using better training data.
3. **Severity Classification:** Classify potholes based on their severity.
4. **Navigation Integration:** Help users identify roads with reported hazards.
5. **Government Integration:** Explore collaboration with road maintenance authorities.
6. **Multilingual Support:** Support multiple languages.
7. **Advanced Analytics:** Analyse road damage reports and identify affected areas.

## Long-Term Vision
To develop RoadGuard into a reliable road safety platform that supports smarter road monitoring and safer travel.

## Status
These are proposed future enhancements.
